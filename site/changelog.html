v0.2.2 12 Nov 2026 3 fixed2 added

Listening that actually hears you

Testing with the Mac's own voice as the speaker showed the recorder stopping after a fifth of a second, or never starting. This release is about that.

Fixed

  • Captures shorter than a word are treated as a click or a breath and retried, up to three times, instead of ending with "I didn't hear anything".
  • Recorder threshold lowered to 2 % of full scale and the stop pause set to 1.5 s, the settings that transcribed correctly in tests.
  • Whisper runs with non-speech tokens suppressed and no temperature fallback, so a quiet room no longer comes back as "[MUSIC PLAYING]".

Added

  • A short high beep when listening starts and a lower one when it stops.
  • A last listen line in the pane: seconds recorded and the words heard, or the exact reason nothing was.
Clausper ready · ears auto last listen: Heard 3.9 s: "add a readme and commit it" last listen: Heard nothing above the noise floor (attempt 3: exit 0, 0.2 s of audio)

The pane now says what happened, so a miss can be diagnosed instead of guessed at.

v0.2.1 12 Nov 2026 1 fixed

Buttons that outlive their budget

Fixed

  • Pressing listen, say again or read this one in the pane ran past the 10-second budget a button handler has, and the engine skipped it. Those handlers now hand the work to a timer, the same way the summary runs.
v0.2.0 12 Nov 2026 2 added3 changed

Listen mode, and speech that starts at once

Two complaints from the first hour of use: the wait before the voice started, and having to type the next prompt by hand.

Added

  • Listen mode. After the summary Clausper asks "What next?", records until you pause, transcribes on your Mac with whisper.cpp, and sends your words as the next prompt. /clausper listen auto|fill|off, remembered across sessions.
  • /clausper setup reports the recorder, transcriber and model, with install hints for whatever is missing.

Changed

  • Short replies are spoken as they are, with no summary call at all.
  • Long replies start with the opening sentence the moment the turn ends, while Haiku writes the rest. Haiku is told what was already said so it does not repeat it.
  • The pane shows how long each summary took.
Clausper is built, validated, and tested. spoken 0.4 s after the turn ended It speaks a summary after every reply and lists the files it touched. haiku summary · 1.4 s What next? │ > add a readme and commit it

The opening line plays first; the summary follows; the next prompt comes from your voice.

v0.1.0 12 Nov 2026 5 added

First release

Built in one session as a Claude Code mod: a hooks module, a state contract and a test suite, loaded live while it was written.

Added

  • A spoken summary of every reply through the macOS system voice, written by Haiku through your own Claude Code session. No API key.
  • The Clausper pane: each turn with its headline, the files Claude edited or wrote and the commands it ran. Press a file for its git diff, to open it in your editor, or to copy its path.
  • A band above the prompt for terminals too narrow to dock the pane.
  • Commands: /clausper, mute, unmute, again, stop, voice <name>, voices, test.
  • When the summary call fails, the reply's own first sentences are spoken instead, so you always hear something.
